# Pick the form

Read this before the grill's first round. Pick the form before drafting.

## Read the collections

Read `src/content.config.ts` and list its exported collections under Settled
in `dumps/<slug>.answers.md`. Match each form to the collection that supports
it; names alone do not decide what a collection holds.

- A note holds one idea, within `WRITING.md`'s note length.
- An essay makes an argument from the owner's own view.
- A letter speaks to subscribers, dated, in a letter's voice.
- Research answers questions someone asked, with sources. Then follow
  `references/research.md`.
- An update is dated news of what changed.
- A page is a standing page, such as about or now.

Offer a form only when the wiki has a collection for it. The starter has
`notes` and `updates`; another wiki may have different collections.
If the material fits a form with no collection, say which form fits and
which collection is missing in the round, and ask how to proceed.
Do not silently put an essay or a letter into notes. Do not create a
collection. Leave collection changes to the owner.

## Write the round

When the material is obviously a note, record `Form: note` under Settled
with a one-line reason. For example: `Form: note. One idea, within the
note length in WRITING.md.`

Otherwise, make the form `Q1`, with the recommended answer first, per
`references/grill.md`. If two forms fit, ask which to use. If the fitting
form has no collection, recommend pausing the draft for the owner's
decision, then ask whether to wait or reshape it into an available form.
Do not offer the missing form as a supported choice.

If the dump names a `target:` in a collection that does not hold the
chosen form, say so in the round and ask for a destination that does.

Write the round, print the path, stop. The draft turn reads the chosen
form from the answered round, including Settled; it never picks a form.
If the form is unresolved, stop the draft and write the question in the
next round.
