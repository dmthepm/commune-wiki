## Leave room to change your mind

Public notes do not have to be finished essays. Explain what you know and what you still wonder about.

[[Connected notes]] make those changes easier to follow. Start again at [[Welcome]] when you are ready to add your own note.

Only notes with `visibility: public` are published. Keep private and draft work in the notes collection; updates are always public.
